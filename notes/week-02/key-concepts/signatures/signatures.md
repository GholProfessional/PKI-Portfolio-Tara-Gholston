Lab 3 Observations

1) Why verification succeeds before tampering.
2) Why verification fails after modification.
3) Why digital signatures require both hashing and asymmetric cryptography.
4) How this relates to certificate signing in PKI.


1. Verification succeeds tampering by observing the Digital Signature’s Hash matched and its Asymmetric Encryption protected the data with the End User having received the ciphertext now opened it to read it as plaintext.     

2. Verification fails after modification because the Digital Signature’s Hash was changed either intentionally or unintentionally. 

3. Digital Signatures requires both Hashing and Asymmetric Cryptography because the data needs the depth of defense when communicating critical/sensitive data internally or externally of the Company/Enterprise’s Network.   

4. Digital Signatures relate to Certificate signing to PKI because it provides Integrity and Confidentiality which builds the trust amongst the End Users/systems/devices and the services internally or externally of the Company/Enterprise’s Network.
